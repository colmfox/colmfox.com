Politics of Southeast Asia

I am an Associate Professor of Political Science at Singapore Management University. I study protest and activism, elections, political communication, and ethnic politics.

My current research is on protest and activism in Southeast Asia. In all my work I build large original datasets from the material of politics itself: tens of thousands of election posters photographed in Indonesia and Thailand, decades of local newspaper reports from across the region, and, more recently, AI-assisted coding of news reports on elections and protest. My book, Bonding, Bridging, & Bypassing (Oxford University Press, 2024), won the Best Book Award from APSA’s Race, Ethnicity and Politics Section and was shortlisted for the EuroSEAS Social Science Book Prize.

Before academia I worked in the US as a design director in online media.
