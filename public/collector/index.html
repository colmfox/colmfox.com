Data collection

Some of my research uses automated collectors that gather published material, such as news articles, from public websites. If one of them has visited your site, this page explains who runs it, how it behaves, and how to stop it.

Academic, non-commercial research. Collected material is never republished, and the data is never sold or used for any commercial purpose.

Who runs them

The collectors are run by Colm A. Fox, Associate Professor of Political Science at Singapore Management University (faculty profile on the SMU website · personal website), for academic research.

How they behave

Every collector listed here:

  • names itself in every request, with a link to its own page;
  • reads robots.txt and follows it;
  • collects slowly, one request at a time, and stops if your site signals that it is busy;
  • never uses proxies, never disguises itself, and never gets around a paywall or login. It logs in only where a publisher has agreed to this.

Each collector follows the robots.txt rules written for its own name and for all robots (User-agent: *).

How to stop them

Email colmfox@smu.edu.sg and collection from your site will stop within 24 hours. Or block a collector in your robots.txt by its name; each collector’s page shows the lines to use.
